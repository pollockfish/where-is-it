## *Where Is It?*

"Am I going to remember where I just put that thing?" you think to yourself as the microwave pulls your attention away with a beep -- and then *you never found that thing again*.

Does this happen to you? Are there things you're constantly losing around the house? Your pair of scissors? The nail clippers? Your audition tape for *Days Of Our Lives* that you hastily pull out of the VCR and hide whenever guests come over?   

*Where Is It?* is a tool for remembering where you put something. Simply send an email to whereisitserver@gmail.com with the **subject** as the **name of the item** and the **body** of the email as **the item's last known location**. You can view your list of items via the web UI.

*Where Is It?* was built using [**Node.js**](https://nodejs.org/en), [**Angular**](https://angular.dev/), and [**MySQL**](https://www.mysql.com/) (using [AWS' RDS](https://aws.amazon.com/rds/) for the  database hosting).
